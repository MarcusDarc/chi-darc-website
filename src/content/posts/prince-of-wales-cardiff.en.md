In Cardiff on business I found The Prince of Wales — a former theatre now run by Wetherspoon.

I like many of their pubs across the UK: they offer a sense of safety for those who need it, and they care about restoring and keeping each building’s character.

Also: their spicy chicken wings are excellent.
