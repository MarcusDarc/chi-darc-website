A few days ago we had an unforgettable weekend.

We visited musician friends in Devon who share our love of art — walking in the woods, painting, playing music, reading poems, and sharing good food.

Making art alone is a joy; making it together, trading ideas, is another kind of thrill.

Here are two short poems I wrote years ago — the first in 2011 before I met Mr Ma, the second after.

**(I)**  
It happened before, it can happen again  
You said I'm your one, I'm your only friend  
But when the night falls and the silence grows  
I wonder where that promise goes

**(II)**  
In the quiet of this room I find  
A hand that steadies heart and mind  
Not every storm must leave us cold  
Some winters teach us how to hold
