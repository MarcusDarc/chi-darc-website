Back in Taiwan I saw the people I needed to see, ate what I had been craving, and did what I had hoped to do — grateful for it all.

For the return, I used Skyscanner’s multi-city tool in two legs: 1) Taipei to Singapore, 2) Singapore to London. It was cheaper than a direct Taiwan–UK search, and gave me an extra day in Singapore.

With the same approach on this trip I also added Milan, Venice, and Singapore. Recommended for travellers who want more places for less.
